Preserving the Legacy of the "Pan" Ma'apilim
Along the Sea Trail

The torch has been passed to us! Honoring the legacy of the Cyprus Ma’apilim along Israel’s shores.

Dear Members of the "Pan" Family,

On these shores, where the waves of the Mediterranean break, one of the most dramatic and inspiring chapters in the story of Israel’s establishment unfolded. Towards these shores, some 15,500 Ma'apilim – our parents, our grandparents, and perhaps some of you – turned their eyes as they departed from Burgas, Bulgaria, in the largest operation in the history of Aliyah Bet.

"Every commemorative sign is a living memorial – a bridge connecting those who walk the trail with the women, men, and crews who were aboard the ships."

The Organization of Cyprus Ma’apilim is working to install 30 commemorative signs and interactive information points along Israel’s coast, telling the stories of all 142 Aliyah Bet ships.

Professor Snunith Shoham, Chair of the Organization of Cyprus Ma’apilim
